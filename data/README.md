# Electronic nose recordings and headspace GC–MS profiles of two palm cooking oils under repeated heating

Two packaged palm cooking oils of one producer, `SA` (premium) and `SIP` (economy), were heated for 5 min at 200 °C once per hour up to 60 h.

`01_raw_enose.zip` holds 70 electronic-nose recordings, `<oil>_<level>-<replicate>.xlsx` with the level in h (0, 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, and 60) and 5 replicates. Each sheet has no header, 8 columns (S1 to S8, mV), and one row per second; rows 0–32 conditioning, 33–158 sampling, and 159–239 purging. `SA_20-1.xlsx` holds only 148 rows and `SIP_60-5_.xlsx` carries an extra underscore.

`02_raw_gcms.zip` holds the headspace GC–MS compound table `cookingoilITS.pdf` and 10 chromatograms `<oil> <level>.pdf` for levels 0, 20, 40, 50, and 60 h; the header of `SA 60.pdf` gives the sample name as `A 60 5MS 15ml`. The Author entry of each PDF is blanked; nothing else was changed.
